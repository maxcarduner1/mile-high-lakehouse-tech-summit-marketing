BRIGHTWAVE — BUILD 2 · HERO QUESTION & DECISION CHAIN
=====================================================

THE CUSTOMER'S HERO QUESTION
----------------------------
"Which campaigns are winning, and why — and how do I replicate that across the
ones that aren't?"

Answered as a DECISION (surface → prescribe → approve → act), not a lookup:
the Campaign Desk surfaces the highest-value *recoverable* underperformer that
has a proven matching winner, the assistant explains WHY and drafts the memo
(grounding the replicate play on the Build-1 Lakebase Search index), a human
approves, and the committed decision is written back and reflected on the next
read.

Environment: the app runs against Angela's Build-1 **production** Lakebase
branch (projects/birghtwave/branches/production). Retrieval for the replicate
play comes DIRECTLY from her Build-1 Lakebase Search index
`brightwave.campaign_search` (BM25 over summary_tsv) — no separate vector store.
