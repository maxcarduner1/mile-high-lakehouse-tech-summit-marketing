BRIGHTWAVE — BUILD 2 · HERO QUESTION & DECISION CHAIN
=====================================================

THE CUSTOMER'S HERO QUESTION
----------------------------
"Which campaigns are winning, and why — and how do I replicate that across the
ones that aren't?"

Answered as a DECISION (surface → prescribe → approve → act), not a lookup:
the Campaign Desk surfaces the highest-value *recoverable* underperformer that
has a proven matching winner, the assistant explains WHY and drafts the memo,
a human approves, and the committed decision is written back and reflected on
the next read.
