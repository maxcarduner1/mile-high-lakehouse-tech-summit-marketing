BRIGHTWAVE CAMPAIGN DESK — BUILD 2 HERO QUESTION & DECISION CHAIN
=================================================================

THE CUSTOMER'S HERO QUESTION (Priya Anand, CMO — non-technical)
---------------------------------------------------------------
"Which campaigns are winning and why, and how do I replicate that across
the ones that aren't — while the quarter is still in play?"

Framed as a DECISION (not a lookup / dashboard): the app surfaces the
underperformers, prescribes the best move per campaign (replicate a winner /
reallocate budget / pause) ranked by projected ROAS lift, lets the CMO approve
or correct, and commits the decision — reflected on the next read (closed loop).

THE DECISION CHAIN — LINKED RECORD IDS ACROSS THE EXPORTS
---------------------------------------------------------
The same decision is traceable end-to-end across the submission2 artifacts by
these keys (values marked <...> are filled from the live run at demo time):

1. view_query.sql / view_result.json
   - ranked live view; CMP-0000214 appears flagged as a top underperformer by
     recoverable_spend_usd. Key: campaign_id = CMP-0000214.

2. assist_log.jsonl  (assistant interactions)
   - EXPLANATION run: request asks why CMP-0000214 underperforms →
     find_underperformer(CMP-0000214) + ask_data (Genie) response.
   - WHAT-IF run: "rank the action, use the model" →
     rank_actions(CMP-0000214) returns replicate_winner / reallocate_budget /
     pause with predicted lifts.
   - Keys: campaign_id = CMP-0000214 ; matching_winner_campaign_id = <...>.

3. drafted_sample.md  (auto-drafted on-brand brief)
   - the replicate-the-winner campaign brief drafted for CMP-0000214,
     grounded on the winner's creative (via search_creatives over Lakebase).

4. writeback_table.json  (writable Postgres app.campaign_actions_app)
   - the committed action row: id = <ACTION_ID (uuid)>,
     campaign_id = CMP-0000214, action_type = replicate_winner,
     target_campaign_id = <MATCHING_WINNER_CAMPAIGN_ID>,
     status = approved, approved_by = <APPROVER_EMAIL>,
     created_at = <...>, decided_at = <...>.

5. state_table.json  (Lakebase workflow-state & observability)
   - the trigger event(s) that refreshed the ranked view (schedule / system
     update) + the recorded decision, each with timestamps; ties trigger →
     decision for action_id = <ACTION_ID>.

CLOSED LOOP
-----------
After approval, execute_campaign_action writes the row above to
app.campaign_actions_app; the Campaign Desk queue re-reads and reflects it
(CMP-0000214 → "Action recorded · replicate_winner", underperformer count −1,
recoverable-spend KPI ticks down) — the committed decision shows on the next
read without a reload.
